# Mission Statement
Our mission is to support local music artists by offering a platform that increases their visibility and connects them with the community. 
We address the issue of limited exposure for emerging musicians by featuring artist profiles, event updates, and more on our bulletin. 
By doing so, we create a vibrant community where local talent can gain recognition and thrive.
